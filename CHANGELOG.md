## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-29

First public release.

- Convert a keymap between two layouts by physical key position, with column and row offsets.
- Layout presets: qwerty, qwertz, azerty, colemak, colemak-dh, dvorak, bepo, workman, neo2, jcuken.
- Custom layouts on a resizable key grid.
- Custom keymaps with modifiers, multi-step shortcuts and locked actions.
- Import and export of layouts and keymaps as JSON, with validation of imported data.
